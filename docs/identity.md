# Identity

Here is how we connect the very minimal visual identity to Dyslex.ai's role, name, and philosophy.

## 1. The Plus Symbol: Extension And Superpower

The overall shape recalls a cross or `+` sign. This directly echoes the nature of the project: Dyslex.ai is a plugin, an extension added to the existing capabilities of an AI agent.

It also reinforces the project's philosophy. Dyslex.ai acts as an amplifier that respects the user as the workflow authority, or the Superpower, by providing clear assistance without replacing them.

## 2. Fluid Shapes: Reducing Cognitive Load

The move from fragmented pixels to a smooth, continuous shape is intentional.

Terminal output, code, and long AI-generated text can feel dense, angular, and hard to decode. The softened angles visually express Dyslex.ai's promise: make instructions and errors easier to read and less visually aggressive.

The continuous shape also represents typo tolerance and the plugin's ability to connect information in a more fluid way.

## 3. Cohesion And Clarity: Separating Order From Chaos

The shape is unified into one solid central block.

This reflects one of Dyslex.ai's core rules: separate facts from hypotheses and make structure explicit. Raw technical text can feel scattered and confusing, especially for dys* or neurodivergent audiences. The logo suggests information that has been processed, grouped, checked for ambiguity, and presented clearly.

## 4. Symmetry And Stability: Visual Predictability

The logo's symmetry creates a stable visual anchor.

For users with dys* difficulties, spatial orientation can sometimes be challenging: letters may feel inverted, lines may be skipped, and visual order may require extra effort. A balanced and symmetrical logo communicates stability and reliability. It does not appear to move; it feels explicit, like the success criteria Dyslex.ai aims to produce.

## 5. Midnight Blue: Technical Rigor And Accessibility

Midnight blue on a light background gives strong contrast while often feeling less harsh than pure black on pure white, which can create glare or a sense of moving text for some readers.

Blue also carries a technical tone: trust, precision, and seriousness. Dyslex.ai is not a childish or simplified tool. It keeps the rigor of the AI agent while making the interaction more accessible.
